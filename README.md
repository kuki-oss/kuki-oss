## Overview

Kuki is your operational intelligence layer that turns historical operational data into recommended actions. Its first shipping capability is restock intelligence, reorder recommendations built on lead-time and demand data, with the same ingestion pipeline and object graph designed to support other operational domains over time.

## Your Operational Intelligence Layer

To see how Kuki OSS looks please see this [features page on our website](https://about.kuki.co.ke/kuki-oss/).

## Architecture at a Glance

Kuki maps a business's operational data into two layers: **Objects** (Postgres: products, suppliers, stock movements, etc.) and **Relations** (SurrealDB: supply edges and other relationships). Restock recommendations are the first thing built on top of this graph, generated by a rule-based engine designed to support other operational domains over time. See [Architecture Overview](https://docs.kuki.co.ke/architecture/overview).

## Editions

There are two products, each available self-hosted or on Kuki Cloud:

- **[Kuki OSS](https://about.kuki.co.ke/kuki-oss/)** our "Community Edition", open source under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).
 
- **[Kuki OSS Cloud (Beta)](https://about.kuki.co.ke/kuki-oss/cloud)** the same Kuki OSS, hosted by us. No feature difference, you're paying for zero server maintenance, managed backups, and uptime, not new capability.

- **[Kuki Core (coming soon)](https://about.kuki.co.ke/kuki-core/)** our "Enterprise Edition", built on the same engine as Kuki OSS, plus cross-industry aggregation, ML-driven models, and governance controls. Self-hostable by large, multi-faceted organizations aggregating across their own verticals, your data never leaves your infrastructure.

- **[Kuki Core Cloud (coming soon)](https://about.kuki.co.ke/kuki-core/cloud)** Kuki Core, hosted by us, participating in our cross-industry aggregation network. The strongest models and ML-driven actions we offer, trained on anonymized signal across every Core Cloud vertical, not just your own vertical.

See [What's Not Included](https://docs.kuki.co.ke/architecture/whats-not-included) for exactly why Core's aggregation can't be replicated by self-hosting Kuki OSS alone.

## Requirements

If you intend to develop (or try and break) Kuki OSS on your laptop and contribute to smarter business tools or build and self-host it.

- Docker and Docker Compose
- Rust 1.94.1+ (only if building from source)

See [ "Dev"(as in developer, not user/business) Requirements](https://docs.kuki.co.ke/self-hosting/requirements) for full details.

## Kuki OSS Cloud (Beta)

We self-host your Kuki Instance on our very own Kuki Cloud. Your specific data remains isolated and only accessible by you, we just handle all the plumbing, see [Kuki OSS Cloud vs Self-Hosting](https://about.kuki.co.ke/kuki-oss/cloud)

## Kuki Core Cloud (Coming Soon)

Kuki Core Cloud is Kuki Core hosted on our Cloud infrastructure, participating in our cross-industry anonymized aggregation network. Where a self-hosted Kuki Core instance can only learn from your individual business' vertical signal, Kuki Core Cloud's models train on anonymized signal across every industry vertical on the network.

This is the one thing that structurally cannot be replicated by self-hosting, regardless of how large your own deployment is, see [What's Not Included](https://docs.kuki.co.ke/architecture/whats-not-included).
